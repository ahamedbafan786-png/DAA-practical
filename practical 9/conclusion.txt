CONCLUSION - PRACTICAL 9: PRIM'S ALGORITHM (MINIMUM SPANNING TREE)

In this practical, we implemented and analyzed Prim's Algorithm to find the Minimum Spanning Tree (MST) of a connected, weighted, undirected graph using the Greedy approach.

1. Greedy Strategy:
   - Starts with an arbitrary vertex and grows the spanning tree one vertex at a time.
   - At each step, selects the minimum-weight edge connecting a vertex in the tree to a vertex outside the tree.
   - Avoids cycle formation by ensuring newly connected vertices have not been previously selected.
   - Terminates when all V vertices are included, resulting in exactly V - 1 edges.

2. Performance Metrics:
   - Time Complexity:
     - Adjacency Matrix representation: O(V^2) across best, average, and worst cases.
     - With Min-Heap / Priority Queue and Adjacency List: O(E log V).
   - Space Complexity:
     - O(V) auxiliary space for visited tracking arrays and MST vertices.

note:
- Prim's algorithm is well-suited for dense graphs where the number of edges E is close to V^2.
- The greedy choice property guarantees a globally optimal minimum spanning tree.
