CONCLUSION - PRACTICAL 12: TRAVELLING SALESMAN PROBLEM (BRUTE FORCE / PERMUTATION)

In this practical, we implemented and analyzed the Travelling Salesman Problem (TSP) using the Brute-Force Permutation approach to find the minimum-cost Hamiltonian cycle.

1. Exhaustive Search Approach:
   - Generates all possible permutations of visiting the remaining n - 1 cities starting and ending at the source city (city 0).
   - Evaluates the total tour cost for every valid Hamiltonian cycle and tracks the minimum travelling cost and optimal path.
   - Guarantees finding the exact global optimal tour without missing any candidate route.

2. Performance Metrics:
   - Time Complexity:
     - O(n!) across best, average, and worst cases, as there are (n - 1)! possible tours to evaluate.
   - Space Complexity:
     - O(n) auxiliary space for the visited array, current path list, and recursion call stack.

note:
- While the O(n!) brute-force method guarantees an exact optimal solution and uses minimal O(n) memory, its factorial growth limits it to small values of n.
- TSP is an NP-hard combinatorial optimization problem with applications in logistics, routing, and scheduling.
