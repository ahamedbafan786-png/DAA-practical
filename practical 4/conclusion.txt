CONCLUSION - PRACTICAL 4: FACTORIAL CALCULATION

In this practical, we implemented factorial computation comparing Iterative and Recursive techniques:

1. Iterative Approach:
   - Time Complexity: O(n)
   - Space Complexity: O(1)
   - Executes product accumulation using simple loop iteration.

2. Recursive Approach:
   - Time Complexity: O(n)
   - Space Complexity: O(n) auxiliary call stack space.
   - Leverages recurrence relations with base cases (n=0 or n=1).

note:
- Iterative computation is space-optimal O(1) and eliminates recursion stack overhead.
- Recursive approach illustrates inductive problem-solving and mathematical recurrence structures.
